Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

At DKWIN, we prioritize the privacy and security of our users. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform and services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Data: When you register, we collect your mobile phone number, password, and financial details required for withdrawals (such as UPI ID or Bank details).
  • Usage Data: We automatically collect IP addresses, device types, browser information, and gameplay history to ensure fair play and prevent fraud.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to manage your account, process instant withdrawals, provide customer support, and improve our algorithms. We also use device IDs to enforce our "One Account Per User" policy.

3. Data Security

Your data is protected using industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption. Financial transactions are securely routed through authorized payment gateways. We do not store your raw banking passwords.

4. Third-Party Sharing

DKWIN does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Data may only be shared with legal authorities if strictly required by law.
